Abstractions

A first-person exploration game with a pixelated, low-poly look. You wake up in an endless ocean of procedurally generated islands.

First launch on macOS

The app is not notarized, so macOS blocks the first launch. It needs macOS 11 or later. Open the .dmg, drag Abstractions onto Applications, then pick one way to open it:

  • macOS 14 and older: right-click Abstractions in Applications, choose Open, then click Open.
  • macOS 15 and later: try to open the app once. Then go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll to "Abstractions was blocked" and click Open Anyway.
  • Terminal, any version: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Abstractions.app

You only need to do this once.

In the game today

  • An endless ocean of procedural islands. The same seed always gives the same world.
  • Six biomes, with mountains to climb and coral reefs to dive on.
  • Villages, camps, ruins and lighthouses to find, with furnished houses.
  • A ten-minute day and a ten-minute night, lit by torches, lamps and fireflies.
  • Fireflies, butterflies and flocks of birds, and a wind that moves the waves and the smoke.
  • Free to download. The source code is public on GitHub.

Coming in 0.6

Version 0.6 is in development and not released yet. It takes the game off the islands and into space. You wake up as a small robot on the planet Kodya.

  • True sphere planets you can circle on foot.
  • Seamless takeoff through the clouds to space, Newtonian flight and warp.
  • A procedural universe of galaxies that opens as you explore.
  • A floating robot companion.
  • Procedural ambient music, unique to each planet.
