Abstractions

Free and open source

A space exploration game in pixelated low poly. You play a small robot on the planet Kodya. Your ship is parked next to you, and every star in the sky is a system you can fly to.

The game today

Walk, glide, fly and warp. There are no quests.

You walk, glide, fly and warp. There is no goal but to see what is past the next star. A second robot floats along with you.

  • One seed

    Every galaxy, star, planet and moon comes from one number. Visiting a system reveals the systems around it.

  • Round planets

    Each has its own gravity, day, climate and seasons, set by its star and its orbit. You can walk all the way around one.

  • A ship

    Take off through the clouds and land anywhere. The flight is Newtonian, with real orbits, a warp drive and an autopilot.

  • Wingsuit and boots

    Jump off a mountain and glide. Fire the boots to climb again. They never run out.

  • Music per planet

    Each planet plays its own generated chords. In space you hear nothing.

  • Open source

    Free to download. The code is MIT licensed and on GitHub.

Play together

Play with friends, as many as your server allows.

  • Pause and open your world to your local network. Friends on the same network find it under Multiplayer.
  • Or run a server on Linux, a Raspberry Pi or Docker. It takes one command.

First launch on macOS

The app is not notarized, so macOS blocks the first launch. You only need to do this once.

macOS 14 and older: right-click Abstractions in Applications, choose Open, then click Open.

macOS 15 and later: try to open the app once. Then go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.

Terminal, any version: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Abstractions.app
